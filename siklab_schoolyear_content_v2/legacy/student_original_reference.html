The 5 Senses!

Let's learn how to observe the world around us.

📖 Reading Time

Hello, Explorers! 👋

Did you know you have superpowers? They are called your 5 Senses! We use our senses to observe and learn about objects around us. Let's find out how!

👁️

1. Sense of Sight

We use our EYES to see. We can tell the color, shape, and size of an object. Is the pencil yellow? Is the ball round?

👂

2. Sense of Hearing

We use our EARS to hear sounds. We can listen to loud drums or soft whispers.

👃

3. Sense of Smell

We use our NOSE to smell! Flowers smell fragrant, but garbage smells stinky!

👅

4. Sense of Taste

We use our TONGUE to taste! Lemons are sour, chocolate is sweet, and medicine is bitter.

🖐️

5. Sense of Touch

We use our SKIN to touch! A rock is hard and rough, but a pillow is soft and smooth.

🤔 Brain Check!

Which sense would you use to know if a lemon is sour?

Science Showdown!

Learn how to experiment and discover how things work.

📖 Reading Time

Welcome to the Lab! 🔬

Science is all about experimenting! We can change materials, test how they move, and see if they float or sink. Are you ready to be a scientist?

🔍

1. Forensics

Everyone has unique Fingerprints! Scientists look closely to find patterns like Whorls, Loops, or Arches.

🎈

2. Air Power

Even though we can't see it, AIR takes up space! When air moves fast, it can push objects away.

⛵

3. Buoyancy

Why do heavy ships float? Because of their SHAPE! A ball of clay sinks, but a boat shaped clay floats!

🤔 Brain Check!

If you drop a heavy, solid ball of clay in water, what will happen?
