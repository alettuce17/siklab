Science Time!
Let's learn before we play
Look around you — there are SO many things in the world!
Some are alive 🐝, some are made by people 🪑, and some are just part of nature 🪨.
Let's find out what everything is called!
1 / 6
🌱
Living Things
Living things are alive! They can grow bigger, move on their own, have babies, and they need food, water, and air to survive.
🐕 Dogs🌳 Trees🐟 Fish🐦 Birds
🧠 Easy trick: Ask yourself — "Can it grow? Does it need food?"
If YES → it is a Living Thing!
2 / 6
🪨
Non-Living Things
Non-living things are NOT alive. They do NOT grow, do NOT move on their own, do NOT have babies, and they do NOT need food or water to exist!
🪨 Rocks💧 Water☁️ Clouds🌬️ Wind
🧠 Easy trick: Ask yourself — "Does it need food or water to survive?"
If NO → it is a Non-Living Thing!
3 / 6
🌿
Natural Things
Natural things come from nature — they were NOT made by people! They exist on their own, created by the world around us.
🌿 Plants🌊 Ocean🪨 Rocks🌋 Volcano
🧠 Easy trick: Ask yourself — "Was it made by a person?"
If NO and it's from nature → it is a Natural Thing!
4 / 6
🪑
Human-Made Things
Human-made things were built or created by people! Humans use materials from nature and turn them into something new using tools and machines.
🪑 Chair🚗 Car📱 Phone🖥️ Computer
🧠 Easy trick: Ask yourself — "Did a person make or build this?"
If YES → it is a Human-Made Thing!
5 / 6
🌟 Let's Review!
🌱Living
Grows, moves, needs food & water.
🪨Non-Living
Doesn't grow or need food.
🌿Natural
Found in nature, not made by people.
🪑Human-Made
Built or created by people.
🎯 Now use what you learned to shoot the right targets!
