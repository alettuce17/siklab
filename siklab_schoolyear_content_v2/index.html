Controller Setup Process

1

Setup Mode

Hold Button 5 + joystick LEFT for 3 seconds.

2

Connect

Join SikLab-Setup-XXXXXX. “No Internet” is expected.

3

Assign

Generate or open the QR for Player 1 or Player 2.

4

Wi-Fi

Enter the internet router or mobile-hotspot SSID and password.

5

Cloud Ready

ESP restarts, connects to the internet, and appears online in SikLab.

About 192.168.4.1

Both physical controllers can use 192.168.4.1 during setup. Each controller creates a separate Wi-Fi network named SikLab-Setup-XXXXXX. After setup, the controller leaves that temporary network and communicates with SikLab through Supabase over the internet.
